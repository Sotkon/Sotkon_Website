About Sotkon

Sotkon is an international company dedicated to the design, manufacture and sale of underground and semi-underground waste systems, holding several patented products.

1995Sotkon brand origin
EN13071European safety certification
GlobalInternational waste-system operation

Simple and efficient is our motto

The first idea for an underground container came about in the early nineties and became a leading-edge solution for the sector.

Since then, Sotkon has focused on developing products and technology that optimize urban waste collection while keeping operation simple.

Values and culture

Sotkon develops innovative and attractive products and services for waste disposal and collection in underground systems, contributing to environmentally sustainable communities.

The company is guided by efficiency, simplicity, non-conformity and integrity.

Design and quality

Products are designed to be easy to install, operate and maintain, with attention to durability, safety, stainless steel quality and anti-corrosion treatment processes.
