Our History

More than 30 years of development, product refinement and international implementation shaped the Sotkon waste-system family.

1990sFirst underground container concept
1995Sotkon launched as a brand
30+Years of field evolution

From concept to system

The original underground waste container idea emerged in the early nineties as a breakthrough solution for cleaner urban collection.

The company then expanded from a single idea into a portfolio of underground, semi-underground, surface and intelligent systems.

Continuous improvement

Sotkon has kept refining technology, intake columns, collection operations and digital integrations to answer different city contexts and waste streams.
